Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 15 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how TruthOS ("TruthOS", "we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website, interact with our services, or use our digital products. By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites remember user preferences, improve functionality, enhance security, and analyze website performance.

2. Why We Use Cookies

TruthOS uses cookies to improve user experience and provide a secure, reliable, and personalized browsing experience. Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Understand visitor behavior.
  • Analyze website traffic.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Improve our products and services.
  • Support marketing and business analytics.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose
Essential Cookies Required for website functionality, navigation, security and core features.
Performance Cookies Help us understand website usage and improve user experience.
Analytics Cookies Collect anonymous usage information to measure website performance.
Preference Cookies Remember language, settings and user preferences.
Marketing Cookies Help measure campaign performance and deliver relevant content.

4. Third-Party Cookies

TruthOS may use trusted third-party services that place cookies on your device, including analytics providers, cloud platforms, embedded content providers, customer communication platforms and marketing tools. Examples may include:

  • Google Analytics
  • Google Maps
  • Meta Pixel (if implemented)
  • YouTube (embedded videos)
  • Cloud hosting providers

Each third-party provider maintains its own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete cookies.
  • Block cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored.

Disabling cookies may affect certain features or functionality of the TruthOS website.

6. Analytics

TruthOS may use website analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our website. Analytics information is generally aggregated and does not directly identify individual users. This information helps improve website performance, usability and customer experience.

7. Advertising Technologies

If marketing or advertising technologies are implemented in the future, cookies may be used to measure advertising effectiveness, improve campaign performance and deliver relevant business content. TruthOS does not sell personal information collected through cookies.

8. Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, visitors may be presented with a cookie notification requesting consent before non-essential cookies are stored. By continuing to use the website after providing consent, you agree to the use of cookies described in this policy.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

TruthOS may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements or our services. The latest version will always be available on this page.
